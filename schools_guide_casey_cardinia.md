## Frequently Asked Questions

### **Q: How do I find out which school zone I'm in?**
A: Contact your local council or visit the Department of Education website. Each school has specific catchment areas that determine eligibility for enrollment.

### **Q: Can I apply to schools outside my zone?**
A: Yes, but priority is given to students within the school zone. Out-of-zone applications are considered based on available places.

### **Q: What are the fees for government schools?**
A: Government schools are free, but there may be voluntary contributions and costs for excursions, uniforms, and materials.

### **Q: How do I apply for a scholarship at independent schools?**
A: Contact the school directly for scholarship information. Most schools offer academic, music, and sports scholarships.

### **Q: What support is available for children with special needs?**
A: All government schools provide learning support coordinators and integration aides. Some schools have dedicated special needs units.

### **Q: How do I choose between different schools?**
A: Consider factors such as location, programs offered, school culture, academic results, and your child's interests and needs. Visit schools during open days and speak with current parents.

### **Q: What documents do I need for enrollment?**
A: Birth certificate, proof of address, immunization records, and previous school reports (for secondary enrollment).

### **Q: When should I start the enrollment process?**
A: For kindergartens, register by 30 June. For schools, contact them in Term 2 of the year before your child starts.
