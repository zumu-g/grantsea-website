### Bluebird Early Education Cranbourne

**Address:** 78-80 Hoysted Ave, Cranbourne VIC 3977  
**Phone:** 1300 692 632  
**Website:** https://bluebirdelc.com/bluebird-cranbourne/

Bluebird Early Education Cranbourne offers innovative early education programs inspired by the Reggio Emilia Approach, focusing on the unique needs and interests of each child. The centre operates from 6:30 am to 6:30 pm, Monday to Friday, providing long day care services that emphasize child-led learning and discovery. Their philosophy centers on the image of the child as competent, capable, and curious, with educators acting as co-learners and facilitators rather than traditional instructors. The curriculum is emergent and responsive, developing from children's interests, questions, and observations. The centre's environment is considered the "third teacher," with carefully designed spaces that invite exploration, creativity, and collaboration. Bluebird Early Education values strong partnerships with families, recognizing parents as children's first teachers and inviting their active participation in their child's learning journey. The centre's qualified educators document children's learning processes through observation, photography, and learning stories, creating portfolios that celebrate each child's unique development. Outdoor learning environments provide opportunities for children to connect with nature, develop physical skills, and engage in risk-taking within safe boundaries. The centre is committed to sustainability practices and environmental education, helping children develop respect and care for the natural world.
