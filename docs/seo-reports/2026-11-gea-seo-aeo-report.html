What changed this month

Window checked: 1 October – 1 November 2026. Everything below is trade-press and vendor reporting, not official Google confirmation unless stated — read it as directional.

Google algorithm & spam updates

The September 2026 spam update finished rolling out on 8 October 2026, the only confirmed ranking-system change in the window (status.search.google.com/summary; trade coverage via Search Engine Roundtable). Independent rank trackers also logged volatility around 20–21 and 24–25 October, but Google's own dashboard shows no matching entry for those dates — treat that chatter as unconfirmed.

AI Overviews, structured data & Google Business Profile

No new Search Central policy post was found in the window. Google's standing guidance is unchanged: no special schema is required to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and markup only helps if the marked-up content is also visible on the page — it does not guarantee a rich result. The one concrete local-search change seen this month is a WhatsApp contact button test in local search/Business Profile panels, reported 23 October 2026 (optimixed.com/platform/google-business-profile — a third-party changelog, not a Google announcement). Separately, from 1 October Google began charging Local Services Ads advertisers for certain missed calls — a paid-ads change, included here only because it affects local lead-gen budgets, not organic visibility.

How AI assistants cite local/real estate businesses

All sources here are vendor studies, not independent research — read the numbers as directional, not fact:

  • A Yext-based study (seen only via secondary summary) reports 86% of AI citations trace back to brand-controlled sources — a business's own website and Google Business Profile — rather than third parties.
  • A cross-industry test of 200 local-business queries across five assistants found only 8.4% of businesses were mentioned at all; Gemini surfaced the most (11%), ChatGPT the fewest (1.2%) (site-business-found-by-ai.pages.dev).
  • A real-estate-specific vendor study (AgentCite, testing gpt-4o-mini/claude-haiku-4.5 directly, not the consumer apps) estimated 40–60% of agent names returned for "best agent in {metro}" prompts were fabricated or unverifiable (agentcite.vercel.app).
  • A separate press release (FlyDragon) claims 91% of US agents are "invisible" to AI search; methodology is not public — treat as marketing, not evidence.

Australian real estate press

No October-dated article from Elite Agent, REB or Stepps on search/AI visibility turned up in this window's search — no confirmed update found for this category.

Free tooling

No new open-source SEO/schema tool was confirmed released in the window. The free tools already in use for this audit — Google's own Rich Results Test and PageSpeed Insights — remain the right (and only necessary) choice; there is nothing paid worth adding.

AI ranking games — a caution

Several vendors (Agent Elite, FlyDragon, AgentCite, Trakkr, Realbot) are actively selling "AI visibility audits" and "rank in ChatGPT" packages this quarter. One of their own guides concedes the obvious problem: no tool can reliably tell you where you rank in ChatGPT or Perplexity, because answers are generated per-query, not retrieved from a fixed index — there is no ranking system to buy your way into. The one lever every source agrees on is accurate, brand-controlled data: your own site, your Google Business Profile, your reviews. That is what this report's recommendations focus on.
