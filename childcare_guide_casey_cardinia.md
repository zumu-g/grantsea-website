## Before & After School Care

Most long day care centres and many primary schools offer before and after school care programs. These services typically operate:

- **Before School Care**: 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM
- **After School Care**: 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM

- Homework support
- Afternoon tea
- Indoor and outdoor play

Contact individual centres or schools for specific program details and availability.

## Enrollment Information

### How to Enroll

1. **Contact the Centre**: Call or email the childcare centre directly to inquire about availability
2. **Tour the Facility**: Schedule a visit to see the centre and meet the educators
3. **Complete Application**: Submit enrollment forms with required documentation
4. **Waitlist**: If no immediate places available, join the waitlist
5. **Confirmation**: Receive confirmation of placement and start date

### Required Documents

- Child's birth certificate
- Immunization records (must be up to date)
- Proof of address
- Emergency contact information
- Medical information and allergies
- Custody arrangements (if applicable)

### Fees and Payments

- Fees vary by centre and age group
- Government subsidies available through Child Care Subsidy (CCS)
- Payment typically required weekly or fortnightly
- Some centres offer discounts for multiple children

## Childcare Subsidies & Support

### Child Care Subsidy (CCS)

The Australian Government provides financial assistance through the Child Care Subsidy:

- **Eligibility**: Based on family income, activity level, and type of care
- **Application**: Through Centrelink/MyGov
- **Coverage**: Up to 85% of childcare fees (depending on circumstances)
- **Contact**: 136 150 or visit servicesaustralia.gov.au

### Additional Support

- **Additional Child Care Subsidy**: For families facing financial hardship
- **Inclusion Support Program**: For children with additional needs
- **Community Support**: Local councils may offer additional assistance programs

## Frequently Asked Questions

### **Q: What age can children start childcare?**
A: Most centres accept children from 6 weeks of age, though some programs start from 3 months.

### **Q: How do I know if a centre is quality?**
A: Check the centre's National Quality Standard rating, visit the facility, read reviews, and ask about educator qualifications.

### **Q: What is the difference between long day care and family day care?**
A: Long day care is centre-based with larger groups, while family day care is home-based with smaller groups and more flexible hours.

### **Q: How far in advance should I enroll?**
A: Popular centres often have waitlists, so it's recommended to enroll 6-12 months in advance, especially for babies.

### **Q: What should I look for when visiting a centre?**
A: Check cleanliness, safety measures, educator-to-child ratios, outdoor play areas, meal quality, and the overall atmosphere.

### **Q: Can I get help with childcare costs?**
A: Yes, the Child Care Subsidy provides financial assistance based on your family's circumstances. Contact Centrelink for eligibility.

### **Q: What if my child has special needs?**
A: Many centres have inclusion support programs. Discuss your child's needs with the centre director when enrolling.

### **Q: What happens if I need to change my booking?**
A: Contact your centre directly. Most centres require notice for permanent changes, and casual bookings may be available.
